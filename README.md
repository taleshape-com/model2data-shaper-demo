# Healthcare Clinic Network Analytics

> **Demo Project**: Showcasing how to pair **[model2data](https://www.jbanalytica.com/model2data)** with **[Shaper](https://taleshape.com/shaper)** for rapid data modeling, testing, and executive dashboard development.

This repository demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow:
1. **Schema & Synthetic Data Generation**: [`model2data`](https://www.jbanalytica.com/model2data) turns a single declarative schema ([`healthcare_clinics.dbml`](./healthcare_clinics.dbml) / [`healthcare_clinics.model2data.yml`](./healthcare_clinics.model2data.yml)) into a fully populated [DuckDB](https://duckdb.org) database and an accompanying **dbt project** (with 83 automated data quality & relationship tests, all passing).
2. **Interactive Business Intelligence**: [Shaper](https://taleshape.com/shaper) connects directly to the resulting DuckDB database to serve an interactive, high-performance executive and clinical operations dashboard.

---

## Quick Start

### 1. Prerequisites
- **Operating System**: macOS or Linux
- **Shaper CLI**: If not yet installed, install it by following the [official installation guide](https://taleshape.com/shaper/docs/installing-shaper/).

### 2. Launch Shaper & Deploy Dashboard
Start the Shaper server, attach the local DuckDB database, and deploy the dashboard:

```bash
shaper -d .shaperdata --duckdb healthcare_clinics.duckdb --deploy .
```

### 3. View the Dashboard
Once the server is running, open your browser to:
👉 **[http://localhost:5454/dashboards/clinic-overview](http://localhost:5454/dashboards/clinic-overview)**

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is defined entirely in SQL in [`Clinic Overview.dashboard.sql`](./Clinic%20Overview.dashboard.sql) using Shaper's custom type system.

### Interactive Controls (Header)
- **Date Range Selector** (`DATEPICKER_FROM` / `DATEPICKER_TO`): Filter appointments across any time period.
- **Clinic Selector** (`DROPDOWN`): Toggle between network-wide metrics (`All Clinics`) or isolate an individual clinic site.
- **Channel Selector** (`DROPDOWN`): Filter by consultation channel (`in_person`, `video`, `phone`).
- **Export Data** (`DOWNLOAD_CSV`): Instant one-click CSV export of the filtered appointment records.
- **In-Memory Caching**: Queries filter once into a temporary table (`filtered_appointments`) for lightning-fast responsiveness across all cards and charts.

### Dashboard Sections
1. **Executive Summary**:
   - High-level KPIs: Total Appointments, Completed Visits, Completion Rate (`::PERCENT`), Total Billed Revenue, Outstanding Balance, and Unpaid Revenue Rate.
2. **Clinic Performance & Growth**:
   - Monthly volume progression stacked by appointment status (`::BARCHART_STACKED`).
   - Monthly billed revenue trajectory (`::LINECHART`).
   - Clinic comparison table highlighting completion rates, no-show rates, revenue billed, unpaid balance, and collection %.
3. **Financials & Revenue Cycle**:
   - Overall collection rate visual gauge (`::GAUGE_PERCENT`).
   - Billed revenue by clinic stacked by payment status (`Paid` vs `Unpaid`).
   - Revenue distribution by patient insurance provider (`::DONUTCHART`).
4. **Operations & Attendance**:
   - Appointment status breakdown (`completed`, `cancelled`, `no_show`).
   - Channel mix (in-person, video, phone) and booking acquisition sources (`online`, `phone`, `referral`, `walk_in`).
   - Advance booking lead time distribution (same-day up to 8+ days ahead).
   - Peak operating hours heatmap and day-of-week distribution.
5. **Clinical Utilization & Practitioners**:
   - Session duration box plot distribution across medical specialties (`BOXPLOT(actual_duration_minutes)`).
   - Completed treatment volume rankings.
   - Practitioner workload, completed visit volume, and average session duration.
6. **Patient & Insurer Demographics**:
   - Cumulative patient roster registration growth over the year.
   - Patient insurance coverage split (commercial payers vs self-pay).
   - Home clinic patient roster breakdown.

## Data Model & Characteristics

The database (`healthcare_clinics.duckdb`) contains 6 staging tables in the `staging` schema:

| Table | Rows | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `stg_clinics` | 8 | Clinic sites (name, opening date) |
| `stg_practitioners` | 40 | Doctors and specialists across 6 specialties |
| `stg_treatments` | 12 | Standard outpatient treatments and procedures |
| `stg_insurers` | 6 | Major health insurance providers |
| `stg_patients` | 1,500 | Registered patients linked to a home clinic & insurer |
| `stg_appointments` | 6,000 | Central fact table covering October 2025 – September 2026 |

### dbt Project & Data Quality
The staging models correspond to the dbt project generated by `model2data`. The project includes 83 automated dbt tests (referential integrity, primary key uniqueness, non-null constraints, and accepted values)—all passing:
- **Clean Joins**: All foreign keys join cleanly across appointments, patients, practitioners, treatments, and clinics without orphan records.
- **Reliable BI**: Shaper queries can confidently join dimensions and aggregate metrics without defensive cleansing or null-handling hacks.

### Business Rules & Modeling Nuances
- **Status-Driven Lifecycle**:
  - `completed`: Populates `checked_in_at`, `actual_duration_minutes`, and `billed_amount`.
  - `cancelled`: Populates `cancelled_at` only.
  - `no_show`: No timestamps or billed amounts.
- **Revenue & Open Balances**: Approximately 30% of completed appointments have `paid_at IS NULL`, representing real outstanding balances.
- **Operational Realism**: Realistic clustering during weekday business hours, typical 1–3 day advance booking lead times, online booking dominance, and steady growth peaking in September.
- **Demo Limits**: Practitioner specialty is not linked directly to specific treatments, and billed amount is independent of treatment type (the dashboard intentionally avoids cross-tabulating these dimensions).

## Reproducing / Regenerating the Data

The dataset and schema are fully reproducible. If you have [`model2data`](https://www.jbanalytica.com/model2data) installed, you can regenerate the exact same dataset at any time using:

```bash
model2data generate --file healthcare_clinics.model2data.yml
```
